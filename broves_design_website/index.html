A little about me

Creativity is more than a skill. It’s how I see the world.

Hello, I’m Manusha Devmith — a 22-year-old graphic designer from Horana, Sri Lanka, with seven years of experience turning ideas into visual designs.

I enjoy creating designs that communicate clearly, feel memorable and suit the people they are made for. My work includes educational TUTE covers, social media posts, promotional graphics and other creative design projects.

For me, good design is not only about appearance. It is about understanding the message, choosing the right visual direction and paying attention to the details that bring everything together. I value creativity, consistency and clear communication throughout every project.
